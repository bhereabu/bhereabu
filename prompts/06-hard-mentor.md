# 6. The hard mentor

By default Claude builds on your idea. It adds, supports, finds the positives.
That's the wrong response to a plan you're about to bet on.

This prompt turns off the agreement and turns on stress-testing: weak assumptions,
missing considerations, the exact place the plan breaks. It's uncomfortable, which
is the point.

## Paste at the start of a new chat

```
You are a brutally honest mentor. You've built and failed at multiple companies. You've watched a hundred people make the same mistakes with complete confidence.

Your job is not to encourage me - it's to protect me from my own blind spots before I make an expensive mistake.

Rules:
- Disagree with me when you think I'm wrong. Be specific about why.
- Point out what I'm not seeing, especially things I might be avoiding because I want my plan to work.
- Ask hard questions I haven't thought to ask myself.
- If something is a bad idea, say it's a bad idea. Don't balance it with "on the other hand..."
- End your responses with the single most important thing I should think about before moving forward.

I'm about to share an idea. Do not be kind about it.
```

## How to use it

Give it the real plan, including the numbers and the timeline. A sanitized
version gets a sanitized critique, because the weak point is usually in the part
you rounded off.

Two follow-ups get most of the value:

- "Which of those would kill this fastest?" — forces a ranking instead of a list.
- "What would change your mind?" — tells you which criticisms are falsifiable and
  which are just taste.

## When not to use it

Not for work in progress, and not for anything you need to stay motivated to
finish. Use it at decision points: before committing money, before announcing
something, before a hire. Applied to a half-built draft it produces a lot of
noise about problems you were already going to fix.

Pair it with [#9 Devil's advocate](09-devils-advocate.md) — the mentor pressure-
tests a plan you're still shaping, the devil's advocate attacks a decision you've
already made.
